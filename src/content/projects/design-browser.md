A modern, open-source browser specifically crafted for designers and productivity-focused users. Built on WebKit for maximum efficiency and minimal power consumption.

## Key Features

* **Design-First Interface:** Gorgeous, minimal UI that gets out of your way. Every pixel serves a purpose.
* **Advanced Tab Management:** Workspace organization and space-specific pinned tabs mean that everything has a place.
* **Vertical Tabs:** All tabs are managed from a hideable sidebar, maximizing your vertical screen real estate.
* **Privacy-First:** No tracking, no telemetry. Your browsing stays yours.

## Why Another Browser?

Most browsers today are built for general consumption, loaded with features that distract from focused work. As designers and developers, we need tools that enhance our workflow rather than interrupt it.

This browser strips away the noise and adds the features that actually matter for creative and productive work.

## Open Source Philosophy

Built entirely in the open with Swift. The community drives feature development, and contributions are always welcome.
